About Us

Global Sun Tech is a forward-thinking energy and engineering company dedicated to delivering integrated, future-ready solutions across three core domains: Intelligent Traffic Systems, Solar Energy, and Electrical Engineering.

Notable Projects - Consulting

Discovery Coin Pvt Ltd

A detailed feasibility study of a 5MW solar plant. The leading idea was to ensure all the farm operations are guaranteed of power for the promotion of Smart Agriculture and all. In this case, the power would supply excess electricity to the Grid.

Energy and Power Engineering Solutions

A 2.5 MW Grid connected plant at Angelo Carter was successfully completed. The project is currently at the EPC stage. EAPS is also an electrical contractor responsible to development of the Utility scale PV plants.

BestPork Pvt Ltd

BestPork is a factory that process pork products into finished and semi-finished products. A feasibility study for a 10 MW solar plant was successfully conducted, phase 1 was completed with construction of 1 MW PV system that is currently supplying the operations with minimum feed to the grid.

Allied Timbers

Successfully designed and installed 2 x 50kw Hybrid Plants at Gweru and Bulawayo. The plant supply the Truss Plant and excess power is exported to the grid under net metering

We offer the best cutting edge Traffic Signal Controllers in the world. Our flagship is the ITC 3 Global, the state-of-the-art adaptive traffic signal controller developed in partnership with Swarco Technology in Denmark. We also have other new generation controllers which however may contemplate the integration of systems such as traffic counting, video surveillance, red light violations and data collections.

Adaptive Controller

Key Features

  • Real-time adaptive intelligence - self-learning traffic patterns and dynamically optimise signal cycles - reduce congestion.
  • Scalable for smart city integration - it has a centralised cloud management system for city wide coordination. It has open API architecture- IoT sensors, smart cameras etc
  • Superior Data and Remote Management - Rich data queue lengths, system performance, manage remotely - diagnose issues, update timings from anywhere
  • Powerful Web-interface - can easily be monitored and controlled from its built-in web-interface that gives the user full functionality of the traffic controller in real-time.

Specifications

  • Maximum phases - 24 single rack, 48 extended racks
  • Detection up to 40 channels of loop detector inputs
  • Power supply: 50w when fully populated, supply voltage 230V ac, accept wide range
  • Conflict/ bad return delay: 0.4 s (as per SABS standard)
  • Modes of operation: fixed time, vehicle actuated, Manual, part time flashing, hurry call, emergency vehicle call, manual flash, fault, flash mode,
  • umber of plans: time of day, day of week, selected weekdays, public holiday, special
